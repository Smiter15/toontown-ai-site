Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

Toontown AI Studio is a creator-operated publishing tool. Connected-platform credentials are kept on the creator's own computer, and approved videos are sent only to the platform selected by the creator.

Who operates this service

Toontown AI Studio is an independent creator project. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to toontownai.ttv@gmail.com.

Information we process

When an authorised creator connects a platform account, the service may process:

  • account identifiers and basic profile information supplied by TikTok, Google or YouTube;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep the account connected;
  • approved video files and the titles, captions, descriptions and scheduling information associated with them; and
  • technical status information, such as upload identifiers and error messages.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate the authorised creator, prepare approved content, upload that content to the selected platform, display upload status and maintain the security and reliability of the publishing workflow.

TikTok data and draft uploads

When TikTok is connected, Toontown AI Studio uses TikTok's Content Posting API to send an approved video to the authorised account as a draft. The creator must open TikTok to review the draft, make any final edits, apply appropriate AI-generated-content disclosures and choose whether to publish it.

Storage and retention

Platform credentials and publishing records are stored locally on the creator's computer rather than in this website. Credentials are retained until the creator disconnects the account, revokes access through the platform or deletes the local credential files. Local videos and publishing records are retained for the creator's production workflow and may be deleted when they are no longer needed.

Sharing

Information is sent to TikTok, Google or YouTube only when necessary to provide the connection and upload features selected by the creator. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Platform providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Website data

This informational website does not offer user accounts and does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Its hosting provider may process routine request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the site.

Your choices

You can revoke platform access through your TikTok or Google account settings. You may also contact us to request access to or deletion of information associated with your connection. Because credentials are stored locally, deleting the applicable local token file also removes the service's stored access to that platform.

Security

Local credential files are excluded from source control and restricted to the local user account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to limit access and avoid unnecessary collection.

Children

The publishing service is intended for authorised creators who are permitted to manage the connected platform accounts. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its platform integrations change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

Email privacy questions or requests to toontownai.ttv@gmail.com.
