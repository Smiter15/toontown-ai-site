AI gameplay highlights, reviewed by a human.

How it works

The creator stays in control.

  1. CaptureThe stream identifies possible gameplay highlights.
  2. ReviewAn authorised creator approves or rejects every candidate.
  3. FinishTikTok uploads arrive as drafts for editing, AI disclosure and final posting.
